About

I’m a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, affiliated with the Center for Language and Speech Processing (CLSP) and the Data Science and AI Institute (DSAI). I’m fortunate to be advised by Dr. Berrak Sisman in the SMILE Lab, and I’m grateful for the support of the Amazon AI PhD Fellowship.

My research focuses on utilizable mechanistic interpretability of audio-language models, particularly how they represent human-centered concepts and knowledge, such as emotion and speech-based diagnostic information. Earlier work explored spoken-language understanding and generation, LLM agent learning, and collective decision-making in multi-agent systems. For my master’s dissertation at the University of Edinburgh, I worked with Dr. Ivan Titov, Dr. Rochelle Choenni, and Dr. Rohit Saxena.

Outside research, I’m a cinephile (2,000+ films and counting) and enjoy landscape photography, swimming, stargazing, and reading ancient history.
