Emotion neurons in audio‑language models

Emotion concepts have been found inside text LLMs. Here we ask the same about speech: where does emotion live when a large audio-language model (LALM) listens, and what happens if we change it?

Hear itACL 2026

Do LALMs have emotion neurons?

Yes. Switch off about 0.5% of neurons and the model loses one emotion while the others barely move. Turn the same neurons up and it hears that emotion more. Random neurons do little.

Speak itAACL-IJCNLP 2026 Findings

Can they make a model speak with emotion?

Yes, without training. Turning up emotion neurons in the language backbone while the model converts speech makes the voice more emotional. Push too hard and the words start to change.

Across languages

Do they work in every language?

Neurons found in one language barely overlap with another's. Pooling the evidence from eight languages finds neurons that still control emotion in four languages never used to find them.

Across senses

Do the same neurons see emotion in faces?

Partly. In three models that take both speech and images, neurons found from voices and from faces overlap most for the same emotion, and changing one set shifts recognition in the other modality.
