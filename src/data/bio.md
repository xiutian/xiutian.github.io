I'm a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, affiliated with the [Center for Language and Speech Processing (CLSP)](https://www.clsp.jhu.edu/) and the [Data Science and AI Institute (DSAI)](https://ai.jhu.edu/). I'm fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Berrak Sisman](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/berrak-sisman/) in the [SMILE Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/jhusmile).

My research focuses on **actionable mechanistic interpretability of multimodal foundation models**, particularly how they represent human-centered concepts and knowledge, such as emotion and speech-based diagnostic information. Earlier work explored spoken-language understanding and generation, LLM agent learning, and collective decision-making in multi-agent systems. For my master's dissertation at the University of Edinburgh, I worked with Dr. Ivan Titov, Dr. Rochelle Choenni, and Dr. Rohit Saxena.

Outside research, I'm a cinephile (2,000+ films and counting) and enjoy [landscape photography](/gallery/), swimming, stargazing, and reading ancient history.
